salut les amis

i'm seeking collaborators in making a certain kinda music of diverse influences: chamber folk, slowcore, electronic, art pop, and (occasionally) traditions of vocal music (sacred harp, georgian polyphony, québécois chansons à répondre).

**i'm imagining music that:**
- takes time for texture and ambience
- rewards listeners with a shift back to relatable song structures
- haunts ethereal vocal harmonies
- feels warm with (mostly) organic sounds
 
the music is at times: 
- slow and patient
- gently moving, driven by fingerstyle guitar
- plodding along with intricate machine rhythms
- building to big ecstatic moments

think of artists like:
- björk
- sigur rós
- múm
- boards of canada
- oklou
- low
- gregor samsa
- sufjan stevens
- fleet foxes
- vashti bunyan
- ichiko aoba

here is a playlist for the curious: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JbDdt_fuqtE&list=PLGTF9HZ-hjdMQMgzZT5VIl8ev9vqVOevc

instruments would be diverse and dependent on what we can make work. i'm already singing, using ableton, playing guitar, piano, sampled instruments, synths, and drum machines. 

i'm open to strings of all kinds, wind instruments, gentler percussion, and definitely more voices. if you play multiple instruments and are willing to sing, hell yeah. 

i have a space to practice in hochelaga and am mostly looking to write, self-produce, and play local shows in intimate settings.
