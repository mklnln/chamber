salut les amis

i'm seeking collaborators in making a certain kinda music of diverse influences: chamber folk, slowcore, electronic, art pop, and (occasionally) traditions of vocal music (sacred harp, georgian polyphony, québécois chansons à répondre).

i'm imagining music that takes time for texture and ambience then rewards listeners with a shift back to relatable song structures, ethereal vocal harmonies, and (mostly) organic sounds. the music can be at times slow and patient, other times gently moving driven by fingerstyle guitar, other times plodding along with intricate machine rhythms, other times building to big ecstatic moments. think artists like björk, sigur rós, múm, boards of canada, oklou, low, gregor samsa, sufjan stevens, fleet foxes, vashti bunyan, and ichiko aoba. here is a playlist for the curious: youtube

instruments would be diverse and dependent on what we can make work. i'm already singing, using ableton, playing guitar, piano, sampled instruments, synths, and drum machines. i'm open to strings of all kinds, wind instruments, gentler percussion, and definitely more voices. if you play multiple instruments and are willing to sing, hell yeah.

i have a space to practice in hochelaga and am mostly looking to write, self-produce, and play local shows in intimate settings.
